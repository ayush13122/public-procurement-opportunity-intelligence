## Important interpretation notes

- Banbasa's 36-month clause is labelled **Defect Liability Period**. It is not silently relabelled as a separate product warranty.
- Odisha is a multi-work tender. A blank single-value EMD or bid-document-cost field is intentional because the schedule differs by work serial.
- Odisha's deadline is intentionally blank in the Bid-Fit scalar field because two explicit entries conflict. The Q&A and review flags show the conflicting page-backed dates.
- Assam's bid closing date is portal-only in the uploaded tender. The issue/IFB date must not be returned as its submission deadline.
- Gemini availability and quota were not part of these offline tests. The local baseline remains usable without a Gemini API key.
