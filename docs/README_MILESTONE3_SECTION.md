## Company Bid-Fit Analysis

The prototype includes a transparent rules-based company-to-tender fit screen. Enter a company profile and criteria confirmed from the official tender documents to see a `POTENTIAL BID`, `MANUAL REVIEW`, or `NO-BID` recommendation with a criterion-level breakdown.

This is decision support, not a tender-win probability or profitability model. Missing/unverified data and source status are explicitly flagged. See [`docs/BID_FIT_ANALYSIS.md`](docs/BID_FIT_ANALYSIS.md) for the scoring logic and limitations.
